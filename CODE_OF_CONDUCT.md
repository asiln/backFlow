# Code of Conduct

We want Figma Lift to be a welcoming project for everyone.

**Expected:** be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, give and accept feedback graciously, focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that is unprofessional or unwelcome.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues or pull requests that violate this, and may ban contributors who repeatedly do.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private contact options on the maintainer's profile or open a private security advisory on the repository. Reports are handled confidentially.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
